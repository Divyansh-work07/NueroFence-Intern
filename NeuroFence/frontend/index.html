Saved scan records

How to read a result

Risk score

A review-priority score from 0–100. It is a rule-based indicator, not a probability and not proof of malicious behavior.

Finding

One observable condition in the file, such as a non-finite weight. Each finding describes what was seen and why it may matter.

Next step

Compare suspicious evidence with the model source and an authenticated, architecture-matched trusted checkpoint before deployment.

NeuroFence performs static checks only. A low score does not certify a model as safe. Behavioral trigger testing is not enabled. Do not expose this development server to untrusted networks.
